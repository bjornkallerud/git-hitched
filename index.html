Travel Information

Getting There

Nearest Airport: Santa Barbara Airport (SBA) — about 15 minutes to the venue

Los Angeles (LAX): Approximately 1.5–2 hours north on scenic Highway 101. Car rentals are widely available, or take the Santa Barbara Airbus — a direct shuttle service from LAX to downtown Santa Barbara.

SBA is by far the easier arrival, but LAX has far more flight options — both work well

Accommodations

Most of our guests are from Santa Barbara, but for those traveling in, we recommend staying in Santa Barbara for the best experience. Here are options ranging from budget-friendly to luxurious:

  • $ — El Capitan & Refugio State Beach (camping)
  • $–$$ — El Cap Canyon (glamping cabins & safari tents)
  • $$ — Motel 6, Marina Beach Motel
  • $$$ — Hotel Californian, Kimpton Canary, Harbor View Inn
  • $$$$ — Bacara Resort & Spa (Goleta), Rosewood Miramar

Hotels on Cabrillo Street are ideal — easy walking distance to downtown, the Funk Zone, and the beach. Goleta is closer to the venue but Santa Barbara has more to offer for the weekend.

Things to Do

  • Dart Coffee in the harbor and go for a morning stroll along the seawall
  • Burritos at Mony's or El Sitio
  • Best coffee: Handlebar, Dart, Dune, Cajé
  • Best brunch: La Paloma
  • Best lunch: Blue Owl, Public Market, Panino, Brophy's
  • Best happy hour: La Paloma, Dusk, Apertivo, Brophy's
  • Best dinner: La Paloma, Barbareno, Loquita, The Lark, Lucky Penny, Downtown Eats, Brophy's
  • Other entertainment: See a film at the Riviera theater; countless hikes, waterfalls, hot springs; take a surf lesson; go whale watching; take a trip to the Channel Islands; live music at SoHo; Santa Barbara Trapeze Co.; rent a bike and loop around downtown; shop on State Street

Santa Barbara gets a bad wrap, but it's actually a pretty nice place to be. Some local favorites:
